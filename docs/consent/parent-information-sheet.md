Thank you for thinking about taking part. Please read this sheet, ask us anything, and keep a copy.

## What is Alo.ai?

Alo.ai is a reading helper for children in Primary 1 to 6. Your child talks with the app, reads a short story out loud, retells it and answers a few questions. We then make a short report for you and ideas for reading together at home.

Alo.ai is a **screening tool that helps us practise reading**. Your child is never shown a score, a timer or a mark, and is never marked down for an answer.

## What is this study?

A small group of children, from families we know, will try Alo.ai so that we can check it works well on ordinary phones. A session takes about 15 minutes. A grown-up can stay with your child, and your child can stop at any time.

## What do we record and keep?

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your child's voice while reading and answering | To work out the reading report | Up to 90 days, then deleted |
| A written transcript of what was said | To check the answers and make the report | Stays in your account until you ask us to delete it |
| The reading report and tips for you | So you can see how your child did and what to try | Stays in your account until you ask us to delete it |
| Your child's first name, class and year of birth, and your sign-in email | To set up the account and show you the right report | While the account exists |

We do not record video or take photos. We do not keep your phone number.
